# **App Name**: EduAI Planner

## Core Features:

- Curriculum Management: Enables teachers to create, edit, and view lesson plans (RPP), annual programs (PROTA), and semester programs (Promes) through a user-friendly interface.
- AI-Powered Recommendations: Integrates AI to analyze lesson content and suggest learning objectives, teaching methods, and related materials. The AI tool assists in dividing materials according to annual and semester needs, enhancing the relevance and effectiveness of teaching plans.
- Data import & Export: Allows users to export documents, AI recommendations and data in PDF, Excel, and Word formats for easy documentation, distribution, and further editing. Also allows users to import data.
- Laravel Filament Integration: Uses Laravel Filament as the backend framework.
- Login Page: Provides a secure and user-friendly login page for teachers, students, and administrators.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Calm blue (#3498db) to create a sense of trust and reliability.
- Secondary color: Light gray (#ecf0f1) to provide a clean and modern backdrop.
- Accent: Green (#2ecc71) for positive feedback, success indicators, and call-to-action buttons.
- Clean and intuitive design with clear navigation for easy access to all features.
- Consistent and clear icons to represent different functions and content types.
- Mobile responsive and modern design.
